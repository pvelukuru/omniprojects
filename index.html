What does Omni Projects Pvt. Ltd. do?

Omni Projects Pvt. Ltd. (OPPL) is a Hyderabad-based renewable energy and solar EPC (engineering, procurement and construction) company. It delivers utility-scale solar power plants, balance of system and power evacuation, 33kV–220kV substations, battery energy storage (BESS) integration, operations and maintenance, and open-access project development across India.

What size of solar projects does Omni Projects execute?

Omni Projects executes solar EPC projects from 3 MW to 100 MW, including turnkey ground-mounted plants and C&I rooftop systems, with a portfolio of 350 MW+ commissioned and ongoing.

Which states does Omni Projects work in?

Omni Projects has execution teams active in 12 Indian states, with completed and ongoing solar projects in Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh and Odisha. The company is headquartered in HITEC City, Hyderabad, Telangana.

Does Omni Projects support open access solar projects?

Yes. Omni Projects supports central and state EPC tenders, open-access land aggregation, clearances and approvals coordination, covering the full workflow from capital planning and debt syndication through engineering, EPC and O&M.

Does Omni Projects provide solar O&M and battery energy storage (BESS)?

Yes. Omni Projects provides solar O&M including monitoring, performance analysis, preventive maintenance and asset reporting, and integrates battery energy storage systems for grid resilience, alongside small-scale BESS manufacturing.

Who are Omni Projects' clients?

Omni Projects' clients include NTPC Limited, NTPC Renewables, NLC India, SCCL, BHEL, NCC Limited, LANCO, Airports Authority of India, South Central Railway and juwi.
